THE MUSEUM

Situated in the historic Fort Kochi, the Kochi Art Museum emerges as a sanctuary for modern and contemporary Indian art. Our architecture blends traditional Kerala design with minimalist contemporary spaces, creating an environment where art breathes freely.

The museum's inaugural exhibition marks a significant moment in India's art history, presenting 59 works by Maqbool Fida Husain that have never been exhibited publicly before.

THE EXHIBITION

Exhibition Preview

A Journey Through Time

This comprehensive exhibition traces MF Hussain's artistic evolution across six decades, from his early experimental works to his mature masterpieces. Each of the 59 artworks tells a story of innovation, cultural dialogue, and artistic courage.

EXHIBITION HIGHLIGHTS

Curatorial Vision

Curated by Dr. Anjali Menon, the exhibition presents Hussain's works in thematic clusters that reveal his enduring fascination with Indian mythology, cultural symbols, and the human form.

Special Features

Includes rare sketches, personal notebooks, and documentary footage that provide intimate insights into Hussain's creative process and his connection to Kerala.
